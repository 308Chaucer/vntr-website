Intel and Execute for private markets

SLATE is the platform our own practice runs on, opening to outside principals in January 2027. It holds the universe of industrial assets, the investors behind them and the limited partners behind those investors, and it carries the execution side of a transaction in the same place. Coverage opens on the subsectors the firm covers and then widens across industrials.

The Four Walls Solutions Index

We track automation inside the four walls of a facility: storage and retrieval, conveyors and sortation, mobile robots, piece picking, and the integrators that install them. We maintain the index because we are asked to price these assets, and vendor marketing claims are not a sufficient basis for underwriting.
