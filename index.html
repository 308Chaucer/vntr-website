The firm

A single-sector investment bank

VNTR Advisory covers transportation, logistics and supply chain technology. Our principals ran trucking, brokerage and logistics technology businesses before advising on them, and that operating background is applied to diligence and valuation on every mandate.

We work on a small number of mandates at a time and take them exclusively. Buy-side exclusivity is defined by subsector, buyer type, revenue band and sourcing channel, so a client knows which lanes are closed to other acquirers while we work for them. On a capital raise, we do not raise for another company in the same subsector for the life of the mandate.

We do not publish tombstones. We work through a network of sovereign funds, family offices and strategics to anchor a process before the wider market sees it. Sell-side work is reserved for companies we have already served on a buy-side or capital markets mandate.

Assets

Truckload, less-than-truckload, flatbed and heavy haul, tanker and bulk, drayage, intermodal and rail, ocean freight, air cargo, ports and terminals, warehousing, cold chain, robotics and automation equipment, and telematics hardware. Technology controls assets through robotics.

Processing

Transportation and warehouse management, managed transportation, load boards and matching, freight audit and payment, factoring, carrier identity and fraud, visibility, back office automation and data infrastructure. Technology controls processing through artificial intelligence.

Agency

Freight brokerage sales organizations, independent agent networks, freight forwarding and customs books, owner operator and driver pools, and vertical specialists in automotive, aerospace, healthcare and project cargo.
