Approach

How we do things differently

A curated list, released in waves.

Broad Auction (Other Investment Banks)

About 200 names at once. Within a week 25 percent know who is for sale, and 70 percent then sign a non-disclosure agreement to see the numbers.

Staggered Process (VNTR Methodology)

The same 200 names, curated down to ~30-50 with the client before anything goes out, then released in waves to pre-vetted serious buyers. It takes longer and produces as many letters of intent or more, from buyers more likely to close.
